momenT tea co.

Identity & packaging design

momenT tea co. is a premium conceptual brand identity built around the daily ritual of tea.

The goal was to design a sophisticated, atmospheric identity that scales seamlessly across physical packaging and high-end digital motion campaigns, reflecting the changing light and moods of the day.

Deliverables:

Logo design · Packaging design · color and typography systems · ads · social content · website · environmental applications.

momenT brand visuals

Concept

momenT believes that there is a perfect blend for every moment. The visual identity centers on sunlight and subtle hourglass shapes to capture different, distinct parts of the day.

The packaging features illustrations inspired by traditional watercolors, focusing on texture and atmospheric light for a premium feel.

Project Outcome

The final identity system stretches across physical products to digital applications, creating a cohesive brand experience. The packaging and logo system were designed to be flexible and scalable, allowing for future product expansion. The digital content and ads were created with a focus on motion and atmosphere to capture the essence of the brand.
